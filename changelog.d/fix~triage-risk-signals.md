- Triage no longer makes a change high risk because its tests are large: the size signal counts only the lines outside test files, so a change is high by size when more than 400 of them change, and its reason says `N changed lines outside tests`. Docs and instruction files still count. The `Risk:` line and the JSON's `changed_lines` keep the total, and the low tier keeps its rule on the total.
- A `## Decisions` section in the spec no longer forces the high tier: the spec's tier is what its Risk line says, and a spec without a valid Risk line gives no prediction, as it does without the section. The spec lint accepts a filled Decisions section at any tier and still fails an empty or unfilled one. The kit-spec skill now gives the high tier as a one-way door or a high-risk signal named in the Risk reason, or more than 400 expected changed lines outside tests.
