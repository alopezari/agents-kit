- Each pull request's line in `logs/quality.jsonl` records its structure: the cognitive complexity the change added and removed, its largest single increase, and the new copy-pasted blocks it brought, from the new `bin/structure-facts --json <base>`. The monthly review-mining job sets two new splits, complexity added (none, 1-9, 10 or more) and new clones (any, none), against the PRs' fixes, reverts and escapes, and the decision page's Signals row shows the numbers, marked partial when a file or the comparison was cut. They are facts only: nothing gates on them. A part lizard or jscpd couldn't measure is recorded as not measured, and `quality-log pr` gives up on the facts after 240 seconds without holding back the rest of the line.
