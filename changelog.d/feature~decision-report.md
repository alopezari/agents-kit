- `reports html` writes one local decision page per branch, next to its reports, and prints its path: what the task must do, what the change does, what it changes for users, what was tested (every check row with its evidence inline, filterable by result), how to test it by hand, and what you must decide, with the PR, CI for its head commit, the stamps and every Open finding, FAIL, NOT RUN, stale or missing evidence and pending step named. It runs no script, loads nothing from the network, reads only the branch's evidence directory and is never published. kit-ship writes it after opening or updating the pull request and again after the CI wait, kit-follow-pr after each run, and both link it first in Review and testing.
- `reports path decision-notes`: the agent's plain-language notes for that page (four headings, ASD-STE100 style), kept beside the PR body and left out of the report listings. `prune-merged` deletes the page and the notes with the branch's other files.
