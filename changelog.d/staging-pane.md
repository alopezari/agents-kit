- In Claude Code, `/flow` lists the staging guide's steps before the merge, each with its result, who gave it and its evidence, and a Pass and a Fail button that record your verdict without a turn. Once validate frees the branch, the pane follows it in your main checkout. Without the mod, `bin/staging mark <step> PASS --by user` in a terminal records the same row; the agent records its own verdict with `--by agent`, and when it overrides yours the row keeps it, as `was PASS by user`.
