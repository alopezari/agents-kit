- Every spec now states its risk tier on a `Risk: <low|standard|high> — <reason>` line, decided before any code exists; the spec lint fails without one, and a `## Decisions` section means high. Triage takes the higher of the spec's tier and the diff's, says which decided, and never lets either lower the other, so the review runs the riskier of the two.
