- kit-ship and kit-follow-pr record each open pull request's quality signals after every push with `quality-log pr <number>`: its size, tests touched and deleted, verify verdict and whether it covers the change as pushed, red check, diff coverage and weakened tests, all measured rather than passed in. The monthly job joins the last line before the merge to the merged PR by host, repository and number, and sets the signals against fix and revert candidates and confirmed escapes per size band, tier and signal, with PRs that have no signals counted apart.
