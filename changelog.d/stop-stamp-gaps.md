- The stop hook and the review stamps no longer pass silently when something breaks: a git call that hangs times out after 30 s and the stop says which checkout it couldn't finish, keeping its edits for the next stop; a Codex patch that only deletes or moves files is checked; `--kind=verify` and `--matches=<branch>` work; a verify report that can't be written is removed and said; a branch with no commit in common with its base fails the stamps instead of measuring from HEAD; and the 20-problem cap never hides a Change-map question.
