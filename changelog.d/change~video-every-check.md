NOT RUN rows need none.

- `bin/evidence-video` now requires a non-blank `--check`, `--case` and `--expected` (exit 64 otherwise), so no video opens without its explanation.
- The decision page labels a replay's tab "Replay of the log", and shows "no video" on a PASS or FAIL check without one, listing it among the verdict's reasons, which makes the verdict Your call at best.
- kit-validate runs every check through `bin/evidence-video` (the full suite as a replay, instead of reusing the verify report), and `kit:validate-runner` and the Bash guard follow; kit-ship records the checks guide's "After the merge" steps marked as the agent's the same way.
