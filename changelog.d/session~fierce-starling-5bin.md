- The decision page is now a control panel for one question: is this PR safe to merge, or is something missing that could compromise stability or the user's experience? It opens on a verdict that comes only from recorded values (Not ready, Your call or Ready, with the rule on the page), then explains the problem, the solution, testing, code review, what isn't proven and your decisions, each open and collapsible. The decision notes have a new shape, documented in the `kit-ship` skill (background, fact sheets, worked example, architecture map, regression table, decisions with options and costs), and the review's Open findings and the validation's `## Not proven` gaps gain impact fields; older notes and reports still render, with what's missing named. Figures the notes name are embedded in place, and an SVG is only ever an image. An animated figure is a `<name>.scene.json` the page validates against an allowlist and compiles itself, with no-script play, pause and restart; a refused scene costs only that figure. A missing verify report now reads Your call, unless the verify phase is off, since nothing else proves the related tests passed.
